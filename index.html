The two laws

Law 1 — Temperature: slurry water strictly 85–90°C.
Law 2 — Grind: coarse range, ZP6 5.4–7.4 (C3S 13–18 clicks). Mechanical compensation: tighter ratios, longer bloom, staged pulses.
CategoryRatioBase tempZP6
The engine treats extraction as a balance: hotter or finer pushes extraction up, cooler or coarser pushes it down. Where you stray from a coffee's baseline it adjusts ratio, bloom time and number of pulses. It is a guide, taste is the judge.
