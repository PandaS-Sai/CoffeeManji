The protocol

Coarse grind stays: ZP6 about 5.2-7.4 (C3S 12-18 clicks). Temperature follows the roast instead of one cap for everything. Hotter or finer pushes extraction up; cooler or coarser pushes it down, and the engine adjusts ratio, bloom and pulses when you stray from a coffee's baseline.
